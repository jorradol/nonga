### 4.3 Safety and quality guard gates

- lead/PII cue guard required
- phone echo guard required

- Thai UX tuning marker required
- no finance guarantee

- evidence capture required
- runSessionLocked (or equivalent one-run lock) required

### 4.4 Stop/HOLD governance gates

- clear Stop/HOLD rules must exist
- HOLD required for public/production/real-lead risk
- HOLD required for secret/PII risk
- HOLD required for checklist incompleteness or test failure
